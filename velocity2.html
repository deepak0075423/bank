Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions?
We've Got Answers.

Everything you need to know about Prosvlop — loans, process, security, and more. Can't find what you're looking for? Our team is just a message away.

PROSVLOP INDIA PRIVATE LIMITED is India's smartest loan comparison platform. We connect borrowers to 50+ trusted banks and NBFCs — letting you compare real interest rates, EMIs, and eligibility in one place, with zero service fee and no hard credit inquiry.
Banks only show you their own products. Prosvlop shows you offers from 50+ lenders simultaneously — ranked by rate, EMI, and your eligibility. No more wasting weeks at branches or accumulating hard inquiries that damage your credit score.
You can compare Personal Loans, Business Loans, Home Loans, Education Loans, Vehicle Loans, and Gold Loans. We also help with Credit Card selection, Insurance (Life, Health, Motor), and offer advisory tools like EMI Calculator, Eligibility Checker, and Credit Score Checker.
Yes. Prosvlop operates pan-India — whether you're in Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, or a Tier-2 city. Our digital-first platform means you can access all services from anywhere in India, 24/7.
Yes. All rates displayed on Prosvlop are live rates from our lending partners — not teaser estimates or outdated marketing figures. Rates are updated in real time and reflect what you'll actually be offered based on your profile.
Personal loan rates on our platform typically range from 10.5% to 24% p.a. depending on your credit score, income, and employment type. A CIBIL score above 750 usually qualifies you for the best rates — starting from 10.5% p.a. with lenders like SBI and HDFC.
Loan amounts vary by type. Personal loans range from ₹50,000 to ₹50 lakh. Business loans go up to ₹2 crore. Home loans can go up to ₹5 crore. Gold loans start from ₹10,000. We'll match you to lenders that fit your exact requirement.
Absolutely not. Prosvlop uses only soft enquiries to match you with lenders — which are completely invisible to lenders and have zero impact on your CIBIL or Experian score. A hard inquiry only happens when you formally apply to a specific lender you choose.
It's a 4-step process: (1) Fill one smart application with your basic details, (2) Our AI instantly matches your profile to eligible lenders, (3) Compare real offers side-by-side, (4) Choose the best offer and get approved — typically within 24 hours with help from your dedicated advisor.
Most pre-matched loans are approved within 24 hours of document submission. Gold loans can be disbursed the same day. Home loans typically take 3–7 days. Your dedicated relationship manager tracks the application and proactively resolves any delays.
Documents vary by loan type. Typically: Aadhaar card, PAN card, last 3 months' bank statements, and salary slips (for employed) or ITR + P&L statement (for self-employed). Use our free Document Readiness Checker to get an exact list for your loan type before applying.
Our AI matching significantly reduces the chance of rejection by only showing you lenders where your profile qualifies. If a rejection still happens, your advisor will analyse the reason, suggest profile improvements, and identify alternative lenders — all at no cost.
Yes, completely. Prosvlop uses 256-bit end-to-end encryption for all data — the same standard used by major banks. We are RBI-compliant, ISO 27001 certified, and your data is never sold to third parties. Information is shared only with lenders you explicitly choose.
Never. Prosvlop does not sell, rent, or share your personal data with advertisers or any third parties outside our lending partner network. Our business model is transparent: we earn referral fees from lenders when you successfully obtain a loan — not from your data.
Yes. PROSVLOP INDIA PRIVATE LIMITED is a registered NBFC (Non-Banking Financial Company) regulated by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI). Registration No: AA-0042857. We are also ISO 27001 certified for information security management systems.
₹0. Prosvlop is completely free for borrowers. There are no registration fees, no advisory charges, no comparison fees, and no hidden costs at any stage. We earn from lenders — not from you. Your advisor's guidance is also 100% free from start to finish.
Zero hidden charges from Prosvlop's side. The loan itself will have standard charges from the lender (processing fee, GST on fee, prepayment charges if any) — all of which are clearly shown during comparison so you can factor them into your decision before applying.
Yes, most lenders allow prepayment and foreclosure. The terms vary — some have zero prepayment charges after 12 EMIs, others charge 2–4% of outstanding principal. Prosvlop clearly displays prepayment terms for each lender during comparison so you can make an informed choice.
